# KRAMA — Sequence K

## Brief and research

A personal workspace that turns captured thoughts into a plan, focused work and sustained progress. Preserve the existing K recognition and Iris palette while giving the symbol a more specific geometric character.

The Monier-Williams entry for *krama* includes order, succession and regular progress. This supports the design interpretation of deliberate steps, rather than implying an unrelated meaning for the brand. [Dictionary entry](https://sanskritdictionary.com/krama/64390/1), [original dictionary scan](https://www.sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0319-krakacapad.pdf).

Microsoft's icon guidance supports a simple silhouette, grid construction, minimal detail, and small-size/light-dark checks. Its guidance is used as a practical evaluation reference, rather than a requirement to imitate Windows icons. [App-icon design](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/iconography/app-icon-design).

IBM's treatment of its own logo demonstrates the value of optical consistency, clear space and positive/reversed versions. KRAMA uses its own geometry and spacing rules. [IBM logo guidance](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/ibm-logos/8-bar/).

## Direction selected before construction

Considered a K/checkmark combination, an ascending tile sequence and a stepped K. The first two lean heavily on common task-app imagery or look like analytics products. The stepped K maintains the existing initial, while its short horizontal landings express the name's ordered progression.

- Palette: Iris `#366BD3`; light Iris `#6290E7`; Slate `#11161F`; Obsidian `#151A22`; Paper `#FFFFFF`; Platinum `#E7EAEE`.
- Mark: an upright and a connected pair of stepped branches. One solid color is sufficient.
- Typography: an outlined Segoe UI Bold wordmark with adjusted spacing, chosen for its open counters and clear uppercase forms. Font files are not included or required to display the exported artwork.
- Layout: left-aligned symbol and wordmark; standalone symbol for compact applications. Clear space is part of the asset viewBox.
- Distinguishing feature: the two horizontal landings on the branches of the K. Surrounding presentation stays quiet.

Upright        Stepped branches          Horizontal lockup

## Construction and evaluation

The mark is constructed in a 48-unit coordinate system. The upright is 8 units wide and 40 units high. Branches have an 8-unit stroke equivalent, 45-degree diagonal sections and short horizontal landings. The minimum gap between upright and branches is 4 units, equivalent to 1.33 pixels at a 16-pixel mark size. Filled-path exports remove dependence on stroke behavior.

The initial draft's longer landings made the branches read too strongly as a bracket. Shortening the landings and extending the diagonal runs strengthened the K silhouette. Wordmark spacing was opened to keep adjacent letter contours separate.

Selection considers semantic fit, continuity with the existing brand, silhouette, one-color reproduction, and small-size clarity. These are design judgments supported by rendered checks, not an automated prediction of brand recognition.

The master assets are native SVG; previews and transparent PNGs are rendered from those exact vectors. Light, dark and monochrome versions use the same geometry. The app icon adds a plain Iris container without making a gradient or shadow part of the symbol.

The live application logo and favicon have not been replaced by this design deliverable.
